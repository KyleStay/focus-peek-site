Quick troubleshooting

  • Confirm Focus Peek is enabled globally and not paused.
  • Check whether the current site is disabled or excluded by allowlist mode.
  • Some private, paywalled, deleted, age-gated, DRM-protected, or embedding-disabled media cannot be previewed.
  • If third-party requests or video embeds are disabled, some previews intentionally fail closed.
Protect your privacy: Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone. Remove private URLs, account information, and sensitive screenshots before posting.
